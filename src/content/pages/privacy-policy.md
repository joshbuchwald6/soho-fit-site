## Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 26, 2026  
At Soho Fit, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

**Information We Collect**

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide, including:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Information submitted through contact forms, consultation requests, or membership inquiries

We may also collect basic website usage information through cookies or analytics tools to help improve our website and services.

**How We Use Your Information**

We use your information to:

- Respond to inquiries and consultation requests
- Schedule appointments
- Communicate with you about our services
- Send occasional updates or promotions (you may opt out at any time)
- Improve our website and customer experience

**Sharing Your Information**

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We may share information with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate our business, such as scheduling, payment processing, email communications, or website hosting. These providers are required to protect your information.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

**Cookies**

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience and understand how visitors use our site. You can adjust your browser settings to disable cookies if you prefer.

**Data Security**

We take reasonable measures to protect the personal information you provide. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

**Your Choices**

You may contact us at any time to:

- Request access to your personal information
- Correct or update your information
- Request that we delete your personal information which we will make every reasonable effort to do so using our third party client management system.
- Opt out of marketing communications
