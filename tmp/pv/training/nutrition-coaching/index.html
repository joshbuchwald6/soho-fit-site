Questions

What does a nutrition coach do?

A nutrition coach provides general nutrition education, helps you understand your eating patterns, and supports you in building healthier habits. Sessions focus on practical strategies, achievable goals, and accountability.

Will I receive a meal plan?

Our coaching does not include prescribed meal plans or individualized daily menus. We can help you understand balanced meals, explore food options, and develop skills to organize your own meals around your preferences and schedule.

Can nutrition coaching help with weight loss?

Nutrition coaching can support your weight-loss efforts by helping you build consistent eating habits, understand portions and energy balance, and stay accountable. Your coach will work with you on manageable changes rather than promise a specific result.

Do I have to train at Soho Fit to book nutrition coaching?

No. Nutrition coaching is available as a standalone service or alongside your personal training sessions.

Which session length should I choose?

A 30-minute session is ideal for focused check-ins and specific topics. A 45-minute session offers more time to review habits and work through challenges. A 60-minute session allows for a more detailed discussion of your routine, goals, and strategies.

What if I have a medical condition that affects my nutrition?

Let us know during your consultation. We’ll help determine whether general wellness coaching is appropriate or whether your needs should be addressed by a licensed dietitian or medical provider. Soho Fit nutrition coaching does not provide medical nutrition treatment and enhanced nutritional needs should be handled by a licensed dietitian.
