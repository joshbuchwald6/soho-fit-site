04 — How it works

Privatetraining,start to finish.

01

Meet

A free consultation and fitness assessment. Your trainer learns your goals, your history, how you eat and how you move.

02

Plan

A program written for you, around your body, your goals and your calendar. Sessions run 30, 45 or 60 minutes.

03

Train

One on one, with cardio, strength and core in every session. Difficulty adjusts as you get stronger, with homework between visits.

04

Measure

Regular InBody scans of fat, muscle and water by body segment, sent to an app on your phone. Progress in real data.
